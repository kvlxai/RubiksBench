Scores

Validity says how close an image comes to a valid, scrambled, standard 3x3 cube: the fewest stickers you would have to recolor to get one (plus any sticker no recoloring fixes, such as one with letters or two colors), compared with random colors. 100% is a valid cube, 0% is no better than coloring the stickers at random (which needs about 4.4 recolorings, because the three hidden faces leave a lot of freedom). A barely scrambled cube or a designed pattern is held in slightly less regard: its validity is multiplied by a scramble factor between 80% and 100%. An image that is not read as a 3x3 cube, or is not scrambled at all, scores 0%.

Who it fools says who would be taken in by each image: every failed check is one that anyone, at a glance, anyone who has held a cube, a cuber looking carefully or only a solver would notice, and an image fools everyone below the first who notices. An image that passes every check fools everyone.

The rubric explains what is wrong: up to 17 points, 1 per check passed, ½ when inconclusive, 0 when failed or blocked (see Checks). An image passes only if it passes all checks. Scores are over generated images; requests that returned no image are counted separately as generation errors.
